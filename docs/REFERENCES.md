# Background: the same barriers across India

Vadodara is not unusual. Reporting in other Indian cities describes the same seven barrier types used in this project. These are links to published articles, kept as background evidence. None of their photos are copied into this repository or shown on the map (they are copyrighted). Everything on the map comes from Om's own surveys.

## Standard to compare against

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways circular specifies kerb ramps at main crossing points with a gradient no steeper than 1:10 and flared sides: [MoRTH circular, 2015](https://morth.gov.in/sites/default/files/comprehensive_compendium_circular/302.22-17.06.2015.pdf). Check the exact wording before citing it.

## Why this matters for the project

These sources describe problems in other cities. They show that the problem is widespread and that photo evidence is accepted by courts. They say nothing about Vadodara. Only Om's own photos and the OSM and Mapillary data show what is in Vadodara.
