Mobility barriers on footpaths and crossings
Vadodara and its outskirts

Field survey by Om Dorkar

About this report

  • What it covers: only barriers photographed on site during the field survey. Unreviewed visitor reports are not included.
  • Severity: High = a wheelchair user cannot pass. Medium = possible but difficult or unsafe. Low = inconvenient.
  • Locations come from each photo's GPS, which is accurate to about 5–15 m. Street names come from OpenStreetMap and were checked by hand where needed.
  • Suggested fixes are general starting points, not engineering designs.
  • Each item links to its exact location. The live map with every photo is at omdorkar.github.io/vadodara-access-map.
