Recent activity

    About this project

    Vadodara Access Map is a free, open-source map of physical-mobility barriers (broken or missing ramps, blocked footpaths, missing kerb cuts, no tactile paving, steps with no alternative) in one small area of Vadodara: Alkapuri, Sayaji Baug and the railway station. It was inspired by the University of Alberta Mobility Mapping Project, which uses camera and GPS data to flag barriers like these.

    • Phase 1 (now): a map, a field survey with geotagged photos, and crowdsourced reports.
    • Phase 2: shared storage so everyone sees each other's reports, and contributing verified kerb and tactile-paving tags back to OpenStreetMap.
    • Phase 3: a small ramp/obstacle detector, trained only on photos whose licence allows it.
    Methodology
    • Field survey: photos taken on foot from public footpaths with phone GPS on. Location comes from the photo's GPS data. Type, severity and note are recorded by the surveyor on site.
    • Severity: High = blocks a wheelchair user; Medium = possible but difficult or unsafe; Low = inconvenient.
    • Mapillary photos are other people's street imagery. They are shown as context and only become reports when someone marks a barrier in them.
    • OSM check compares mapped footways and crossings against the OpenStreetMap tags kerb, wheelchair and tactile_paving.
    Limitations
    • Sample pins are invented examples placed at approximate spots. They are labelled and are not findings.
    • "Added by you" reports stay in your browser until exported. There is no shared database yet.
    • Very few footways and crossings in this area are mapped in OpenStreetMap, so the OSM check mostly shows missing data, not missing ramps.
    • Satellite imagery cannot show a broken ramp. It is context only.
    • Phone GPS can be off by 5–15 m.
    • The survey covers a few streets, not the whole city.
